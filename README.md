# 50 Threat Intelligence and Threat Hunting Projects

A hands-on defensive portfolio covering open-source intelligence, indicator analysis, structured threat intelligence, MITRE ATT&CK, SIEM hunting, detection engineering, and enterprise threat intelligence operations.

## Project structure

- **Beginner:** 15 projects
- **Intermediate:** 20 projects
- **Advanced:** 15 projects
- **Progress:** Beginner 15/15 · Intermediate 20/20 · Advanced 15/15 · Overall 50/50

Every project includes a practical walkthrough and three project-specific simulated SVG training illustrations. Illustrations are conceptual learning aids—not screenshots, collected evidence, or claims of real-world findings.

## Safety and responsible use

Use only public sources, systems you own, or environments for which you have explicit written authorization. Use synthetic telemetry and benign sample data. Do not collect credentials, target private individuals, access systems without authorization, or deploy malware. Treat indicators as leads requiring validation and context—not proof of compromise. Respect source terms, privacy, retention, and sharing restrictions.
